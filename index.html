GlowFit — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 28, 2026

1. The Short Version

GlowFit keeps your health data on your phone. There is no account, no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, and no crash reporting. We do not sell anything about you to anyone.

Two things can leave your device, and only when you choose to use them:

  • Photos you save in Progress Photos or the Food Photo Journal.
  • AI prompts, when you use the AI Coach, AI Planner, or AI Insights.

Everything else stays on your phone. This policy replaces the policy dated July 19, 2026, which described a different application and stated that no data left your device. That was not accurate.

2. Who This App Is For

GlowFit is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

3. What Stays On Your Device

GlowFit is local-first. Your weight logs, body measurements and body composition, meal and calorie records, food photo notes, workout history, water intake, sleep entries, mood and wellness check-ins, cycle logs (phase, flow and symptoms), fasting periods, journal notes, your medication list and dose records including GLP-1, and profile details are stored only on your device.

We do not upload this information to our servers. It is also excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so it does not leave your phone through a backup either.

Your cycle records (phase, flow and symptoms) are never transmitted by the app. The AI features do not read them; the only way they reach an AI service is if you type that information into a prompt yourself. Your full medication list and dose history also stay on your device, although the most recent GLP-1 dose summary described in section 4.2 is included in AI context.

4. What Leaves Your Device

4.1 Photos. When you save a progress photo or a food photo, the image is uploaded to our storage service (Cloudflare R2) so the app can display it again later. Each upload is stored under a random identifier generated for your installation, not your name, and remains there until you delete it.

4.2 AI features. When you use the AI Coach, AI Planner, or AI Insights, the text you enter is sent to an AI service. Along with your message, these features automatically build a context summary that can include: your name if you entered one, your age, gender, height, current weight, goal weight, goal, activity level, and whether you use GLP-1 medication; and if you have logged it, the name, dose and date of your most recent GLP-1 dose together with your appetite and nausea ratings for it. Recent workout, calorie, weight, water and sleep entries are also summarised, depending on which feature you use. The AI Coach additionally receives your ten most recent chat messages.

  • Default mode: your prompt is relayed through our proxy to the AI provider configured on it (currently a DeepSeek model).
  • Bring-your-own-key mode: if you add your own API key, requests go directly from your device to the provider you selected (for example Google Gemini, NVIDIA NIM, or Kilo Gateway), under that provider's privacy policy. Your key is stored on your device and is never sent to us.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Photo storage: Cloudflare R2, on our own account.
  • AI processing: in default mode our proxy relays prompts to its configured provider; in bring-your-own-key mode, the provider you configure (Google Gemini, NVIDIA NIM, or Kilo Gateway).
  • Embedded videos: exercise demonstration videos are embedded from YouTube using youtube-nocookie.com. When a video or thumbnail loads, Google receives that request and any playback information, under the Google Privacy Policy. We do not control what Google collects.

There is no advertising SDK (no Google AdMob), no analytics SDK (no Firebase Analytics), and no crash reporting SDK (no Firebase Crashlytics) in this app.

6. Health Connect

If you connect Health Connect, GlowFit requests read-only access to three data types: steps, heart rate, and sleep. We do not request, and the app cannot read, other categories such as blood glucose, blood pressure, body temperature, body fat, or weight.

Health Connect data is summarised inside the app and stored only on your device; we do not upload it to our servers. You can revoke access at any time in the Health Connect settings on your device, or from the Health Sync section of GlowFit Settings.

7. What We Do Not Collect

  • No advertising or ad-network SDKs, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No location, contacts, calendar, or microphone access.
  • No account, email address, or password — the app has no sign-in.

8. Keeping Your Data Safe

Data that leaves your device is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS. Server-side photos are stored under a random per-install identifier rather than your name or email address.

One limitation you should know about. That identifier is a device scope, not a login. Reinstalling the app or clearing app data generates a new identifier, so photos uploaded by an earlier installation can no longer be reached from the app and will remain in our storage until you email us to remove them. Please include the approximate dates you used the app so we can locate the old identifier.

9. Export

You can export all of your data at any time from Settings using Export Data, as JSON or CSV.

10. Deletion

In-app: Settings → "Delete All Data" removes everything stored on your device and deletes your uploaded progress and food photos from our servers. The app tells you if the server-side deletion could not be completed, for example while offline, so you can retry.

By email: if you have uninstalled the app, or the in-app deletion failed, email glowfit.app@gmail.com and we will remove your stored photos manually. We respond to deletion requests within 30 days.

Deleting your data does not remove information already held by third parties, such as your AI provider or Google. Please refer to their privacy policies and account settings.

11. Children's Privacy

GlowFit is not intended for anyone under 18, is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has uploaded photos through the app, contact us and we will delete them.

12. Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page and in the app, together with a new effective date. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
