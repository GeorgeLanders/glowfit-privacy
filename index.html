GlowFit — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 19, 2026

1. Introduction

GlowFit ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the data you voluntarily provide:

  • Health Data: Workouts, meals, sleep, mood, journal entries, body measurements, progress photos
  • Account Data: Name, age, weight, height, fitness goals
  • Usage Data: App interactions, feature usage (via Firebase Analytics)
  • Device Data: Device type, OS version, crash reports (via Firebase Crashlytics)

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Provide personalized AI coaching and fitness recommendations
  • Track your fitness progress over time
  • Improve app performance and fix bugs
  • Display relevant advertisements (via Google AdMob)

4. Data Storage & Security

  • All health data is stored locally on your device, encrypted with AES-256-GCM encryption
  • Database is protected with SQLCipher encryption
  • Encryption keys are stored in Android Keystore (hardware-backed)
  • AI conversations are processed via Google Gemini and are not stored on our servers

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob: Displays advertisements. See Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase: Analytics and crash reporting. See Firebase Privacy
  • Google Gemini: AI processing for coaching features

6. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, trade, or share your personal health data with any third parties. Your data stays on your device.

7. Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time:

  • In-app: Go to Settings → "Delete All My Data"
  • This permanently removes all health logs, journal entries, progress photos, and account data from your device

8. Children's Privacy

GlowFit is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes will be posted here with an updated effective date.
